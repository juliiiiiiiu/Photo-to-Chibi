# Photo to Chibi privacy policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Photo to Chibi is a skills-only plugin. The publisher does not operate a server for the plugin and does not receive, store, or sell user photos, prompts, or generated images.

## Data processed

The plugin can process the following data when you provide it:

- Photos that you upload for transformation.
- Prompts and style choices that describe the requested result.
- Generated images and temporary animation frames created for your request.

Do not upload passwords, authentication secrets, government identifiers, payment card data, or protected health information.

## How data is used

The host uses the supplied photo and prompt only to create the requested chibi image or animation. The plugin instructions tell the host not to reuse current-request photos or generated frames in another request.

## Data recipients

The publisher does not receive plugin data. OpenAI and the image-generation services available through ChatGPT or Codex may process the data to complete your request. Their privacy policies and account settings govern that processing.

## Retention

The publisher retains no plugin data. OpenAI may retain conversations, uploaded files, and generated content under the terms and settings that apply to your account. Temporary animation frames should remain transient and should not be added to the published plugin package.

## Your controls

You can choose not to upload a photo. You can also delete the relevant conversation or files through your OpenAI account controls and uninstall the plugin. Only upload a photo when you have permission from every identifiable person in it.

## Children

The plugin does not knowingly collect data from children. A parent or legal guardian must authorize the upload and transformation of a child's photo when required by applicable law.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository. To ask a privacy question, open an issue in the [Photo to Chibi repository](https://github.com/juliiiiiiiu/Photo-to-Chibi/issues).
