# Japanese anime night

Use this reference for nighttime, dusk, and evening scenes in any location. Render a lucid Japanese-anime night with deep blue-violet ambient colour, clear silhouettes, selective window or street light, and restrained cel-painted light falloff.

Let one illuminated environmental anchor establish the setting, such as a window wall, streetlight, station entrance, shoreline reflection, or distant building mass. Preserve facial readability with controlled local light and avoid placing the brightest light directly behind faces.

Avoid neon overload, heavy bloom, featureless black shadows, uniform orange grading, and photographic night-noise texture.
