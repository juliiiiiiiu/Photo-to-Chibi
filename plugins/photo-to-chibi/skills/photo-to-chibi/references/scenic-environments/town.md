# Japanese anime town

Use this reference for streets, stations, shopfronts, residential neighbourhoods, and city views. Render a clean, high-definition Japanese-anime town with orderly perspective, crisp architectural planes, cool clear air, and restrained cel-painted local colour.

Choose one everyday visual anchor such as a quiet street, station approach, apartment facade, shopfront rhythm, utility poles and overhead wires, or a crosswalk. Keep signs abstract or illegible. Use lived-in detail sparingly; do not add vehicles, pedestrians, logos, or landmarks unless requested.

For rain, show narrow wet-ground reflections and softened distant contrast. For golden hour or night, keep warm lights selective against cool blue-violet shadows; avoid neon overload, bloom, murky blacks, and global orange grading. Keep the characters' faces and hands clearer and higher-contrast than the architecture behind them.
