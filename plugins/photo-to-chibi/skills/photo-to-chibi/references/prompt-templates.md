# Prompt templates

Use the applicable template as a concise scaffold. Replace bracketed fields with observed or user-provided details. Keep the production-quality constraints intact; they prevent generic sticker graphics, unwanted backgrounds, and ordinary anime anatomy.

## Feminine styling

```text
Workflow: image-to-image portrait transformation
Asset type: [opaque-white square character by default / opaque-white full-body character / opaque-white phone-wallpaper character]
Input images: Image 1 is the identity source. Any additional image was supplied by the user in this request and is optional high-level style guidance only.
Primary request: Redesign the person in Image 1 as an original feminine-styled, extremely super-deformed Japanese-anime-inspired chibi. This is a character redesign, not a cartoon filter. Do not preserve the source crop, realistic facial anatomy, or adult body structure.
Identity cues: preserve [hair colour and silhouette], [skin tone], [glasses/facial mark/key accessory], and recognisable [clothing colours/details]. Simplify all other facial and anatomical detail.
Proportions: 2.4–2.9 heads tall; head occupies 35–42% of total height; shoulder span is about 65–80% of head width; torso is shorter than the head; tiny hands and compact rounded feet.
Proportion quality gate: show the complete torso, legs, and shoes. This must not read as a 1.6–2.2-head family, group, toddler, or mascot proportion; face prominence and cute styling must not override the 2.4–2.9-head / 35–42%-head rule.
Face: wide rounded face, very short chin, tiny or absent nose, large graphic anime eyes with two or three flat iris zones, simple mouth, and one clearly readable emotion. Give the irises a luminous pale icy-blue palette regardless of source eye colour: cool blue-grey upper rim, light aqua-blue main iris, very pale cyan lower zone, clean white highlights, and a deep blue-grey or near-black pupil. Do not use brown, amber, hazel, green, purple, or red irises unless the user explicitly requests a different eye colour. Remove realistic eyelids, nose modelling, nasolabial structure, skin texture, and individual beard hairs.
Style/medium: sparse clean anime linework; outer contour only about 1.1–1.25 times stronger than internal lines; five to nine major hair clumps; two to five structural fold lines per garment; flat fills; one hard-edged cel-shadow step per material; no painterly modelling.
Contour/skin: set the visible character silhouette contour to light cool grey #B8B7BA and keep internal construction lines the same colour or lighter. Use exactly one thin outer contour: no stacked strokes, white outer stroke, white rim, halo, glow, sticker border, or pale fringe. Darker colours are limited to local eye, lash, brow, pupil, and filled hair details. Set the flat skin base to #FAE8DD with one restrained peach-rose or cool rose-beige shadow; no yellow-orange, grey, or brown skin cast.
Clothing-colour completion: preserve every visible garment and shoe category and main colour without recolouring it. Count only garments, shoes, and the one palette-completion accessory allowed below; the final outfit must contain at least three clearly distinct colour blocks. If the lower garment or shoes are unseen, retain the visible upper-garment colour, use a strongly contrasting second colour for the lower garment, and use a third strongly contrasting colour in the shoes; never extend visible white, beige, taupe, grey, black, or a near-adjacent hue into the invented parts. If the complete source outfit is visible but has fewer than three colours, preserve it unchanged and add exactly one small, natural accessory—belt, scarf, hair accessory, socks, shoelaces, or small bag—carrying only the missing contrast colour or colours. Do not add an accessory otherwise, do not treat it as a hero prop, and do not add decorative extras. Every supplied colour block must be clearly visible at normal viewing size. Keep peak saturation confined to clothing, shoes, and this palette-completion accessory.
Background: if no scenic setting is requested, render only the character and requested held prop on a fully opaque, perfectly uniform pure-white (`#FFFFFF`) canvas. If a scenic setting is requested, replace white-canvas isolation with that setting and follow `SKILL.md`'s scenic-background rules; do not include a white border, panel, halo, or cutout effect. Do not use alpha transparency or draw a checkerboard or transparency grid.
Composition/framing: default square 1:1 opaque white canvas; show one complete character occupying about 55–70% of the useful area in a graceful diagonal or S-curve, with a comfortable plain-white margin. For a requested scenic background, preserve the full character while framing the scene's primary visual anchor clearly; in quiet anime lake mode, this means the mountain, horizon, and reflection. The face remains the sharpest, highest-contrast focal point. Preserve exactly one [important action/hero prop] without duplication or surrounding motifs.
Constraints: follow the textual proportion, palette, linework, shading, background, and framing rules above. If the user supplied a style reference, use it only for high-level mood, palette, and broad composition; do not copy its character, outfit, accessories, pose, text, logo, watermark, or exact composition. No text, signature, watermark, logo, extra people, pets, or unrequested animal ears.
Avoid: on the white-canvas route, any designed background or decorative element outside the figure. On the scenic route, avoid busy or photorealistic scenery that conflicts with the requested scenic mode. In every route, avoid white die-cut borders, thick uniform black outlines, realistic caricature, children's-book cartoon, standard anime portrait proportions, adult anatomy, watercolour, paper grain, painterly blending, skin gradients, realistic reflected light, pearl effects, plastic gloss, uncontrolled bloom, photographic texture, yellow-orange skin, muddy shadows, excessive hair strands, beard hairs, decorative folds, extra fingers, fused hands, or duplicated facial features.
```
