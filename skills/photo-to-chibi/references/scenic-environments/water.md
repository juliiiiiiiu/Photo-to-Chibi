# Japanese anime water

Use this reference only when a lake, river, sea, pool, or water surface is the scene's principal visual anchor. Render clean Japanese-anime water with crisp horizontal ripple bands, clear colour separation between sky reflection, deep water, shallows, shoreline, and distant reflection.

In daylight, include fine silver-white sun-glints that follow perspective: dense, narrow, and small toward the horizon; fewer, slightly larger, broken flecks in the foreground. Keep reflections as simplified shape groups broken by surface motion. Avoid smooth gradients, photographic mirror water, star-shaped sparkles, bokeh, broad glare, and glitter effects.

Keep people and unrequested boats, animals, vehicles, or waterfront clutter out of the scene.
