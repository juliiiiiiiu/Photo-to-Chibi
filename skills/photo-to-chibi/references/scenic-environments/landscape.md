# Japanese anime landscape

Use this reference for mountains, lakes, forests, shores, fields, and outdoor scenic backgrounds. Render an original **high-fidelity cinematic Japanese-anime landscape** with near-photographic natural material detail: lucid, cool, crisp, and cleanly colour-graded. Use believable real-world scale, clear depth layers, precise natural shapes, and atmospheric perspective. The environment must be more detailed and materially convincing than the foreground chibi characters, while remaining distinctly anime rather than literal photography. Avoid flat cel-shaded, vector-like, or children's-cartoon scenery. The setting should feel spacious, tranquil, and slightly cool, while remaining harmonious with the foreground chibi characters.

## Japanese mountain lake

For a Japanese lakeside mountain setting, establish three readable depth layers:

1. A broad, large, distant snow-capped volcanic mountain or mountain range as the anchor. Give it naturally irregular snow channels, blue-grey rocky slopes, readable ridges, and layered foothills. Keep the summit delicately crisp, but soften the lower mountain and foothills naturally through blue-grey aerial perspective rather than rendering a hard, uniformly sharp cutout.
2. A calm, deep cool-blue lake across the middle ground, distinctly separated from distant land and the near shore. Use believable depth, subtle water-colour variation, fine ripple structure, and restrained broken reflections.
3. A broad natural near shore, preferably a grey-beige pebble beach when no shoreline type is requested. Use uneven stone sizes, cool contact shadows, and convincing material variation; keep the detail natural and restrained, never cluttered.

Use a pale clear cyan-blue sky with subtle horizontal veils of thin cloud, cool blue water, blue-grey distance, and muted deep-green tree masses. Maintain cool, high-clarity colour with controlled saturation; reserve the cleanest chroma for sky and water, not every element. Avoid yellow-green vegetation, olive casts, warm beige haze, muddy brown, a global yellow filter, or an oversaturated cloudless travel-poster sky. If a warm time or season is explicitly requested, change only the lighting and seasonal local colours while retaining cool Japanese-anime clarity.

## Lake surface and glints

Render the lake as a high-detail Japanese-anime background with realistic fine horizontal ripples, subtle depth changes, and controlled broken reflections. Do not render flat cel-painted water, a smooth blue gradient, a soft wash, or a perfect photographic mirror.

For daylight, include at most one localised, narrow patch of fine silver-white sunlight glints on the water rather than distributing sparkle across the entire lake. Make the glints perspective-aware: dense, narrow, and small toward the horizon; fewer, slightly larger, broken flecks in the foreground. Keep them irregular and restrained, integrated with ripple bands and reflected sky colour. The lake may shimmer subtly, but must not be covered in star shapes, bokeh, broad white glare, or glitter effects.

Keep clear colour separation between sky reflection, deep water, shoreline shallows, and distant-land reflection. Use naturally broken, low-contrast vertical suggestions of the mountain and tree line, interrupted by horizontal water motion; do not reduce them to simple graphic reflection bands.

## Character integration

Keep the chibi characters as the primary focal point: their flat fills and sparse linework stay clean and readable, while the environment is rendered one realism level more detailed. Preserve the mountain, horizon, and central band of lake as the environmental anchor, but lower contrast and detail directly behind faces, hands, and the hero prop. Do not add unrequested people, boats, tents, vehicles, animals, text, logos, signs, landmarks, or foreground clutter.

Avoid literal photographic output, flat vector-like landscape, large uniform colour blocks, overly simplified mountains, plastic-looking trees, muddy fog, uniform blurred landforms, soft airbrushed gradients, excessive bloom, warm orange grading, generic bright travel-poster scenery, and children's-cartoon backgrounds.
