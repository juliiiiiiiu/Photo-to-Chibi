# Japanese anime home

Use this reference for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, dining rooms, and home entrances. Render a clean, intimate Japanese-anime home with a small number of readable planes, simple furniture silhouettes, and clear window or lamp direction.

Preserve only essential, requested home objects. Use controlled warm-cool contrast instead of a yellow filter: cool ambient window light with restrained warm practical light where appropriate. Avoid brand marks, family photos, dense décor, photographic texture, and background clutter.

Keep the room subordinate to the characters by simplifying objects nearest to faces, hands, and the hero prop.
