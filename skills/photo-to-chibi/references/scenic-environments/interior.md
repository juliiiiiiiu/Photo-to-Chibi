# Japanese anime public interior

Use this reference for cafés, libraries, studios, galleries, and other public indoor spaces. Render a clean, high-definition Japanese-anime interior with readable foreground, middle ground, and background planes, orderly perspective, and restrained cel-painted directional light.

Choose a few setting-defining forms such as tables, shelves, a counter, windows, or hanging lights. Keep text illegible or omitted, and do not add brands, staff, customers, products, or decorative clutter unless requested.

Keep the strongest contrast on the characters; use quieter edges and lower saturation in the surrounding space.
