# Japanese anime school

Use this reference for school gates, classrooms, corridors, courtyards, playgrounds, and tree-lined school approaches. Render a clear, cool Japanese-anime school setting with simple architectural planes, window-light patterns, one strong depth cue, and clean cel-painted light.

Choose only the requested or source-supported setting. Keep classrooms, corridors, gates, and outdoor areas readable but uncluttered. Do not invent uniforms, classmates, school emblems, timetables, or legible text.

Use the environment to frame the characters rather than compete with them: lower detail behind faces and gestures, preserve open negative space, and avoid dense desks, lockers, posters, or sports equipment.
